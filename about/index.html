We built Simple Floww for the businesses big software forgets

Simple Floww gives small and medium businesses in India the official WhatsApp Business API, with the tools to use it properly, at a price a 5-person team can pay.

A note from our founder

I'm Abhinandan Kumar, and I started Simple Floww after seeing the same thing again and again. Small businesses get most of their leads on WhatsApp, but the tools to manage those chats were built and priced for large companies.

A property dealer with a team of four doesn't need an enterprise contract. They need every enquiry answered quickly, a simple way to follow up, and a bill they can understand. That's why we charge Meta's message rates with no markup, and why our team helps with setup in Hindi and English.
