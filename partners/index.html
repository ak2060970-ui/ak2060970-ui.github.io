FAQ

Partner questions

Are these programs free to join?

No. Both are paid programs. We share the joining fee and terms on a call.

What's the difference between the two?

With white-label, the platform carries your brand and you look after your own clients. As a Simple Floww reseller, you sell our plans under our brand, and we handle demos, setup and support.

How much can I earn?

White-label partners pay Simple Floww a separate partner fee and charge their clients whatever they choose, so the difference is theirs. Resellers earn a fixed percentage commission on every Simple Floww plan they sell. We share exact numbers on a call.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. White-label partners get training on the platform. Resellers only need to find interested businesses, and our team takes it from there.

Will white-label clients see Simple Floww?

No. The platform shows your brand and runs on your domain.

Is it the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. Both programs run on the same official API that Simple Floww uses as a Meta Tech Provider.
