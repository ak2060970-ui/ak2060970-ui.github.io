Welcome to Simple Floww. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect data when you visit our website, use our WhatsApp API services, automation services, marketing tools, or interact with us in any way.

By using our website or services, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of data:

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone / WhatsApp number
  • Business details
  • Communication details

Technical & Usage Data

  • IP address
  • Browser type & device information
  • Website usage & interaction data
  • Cookies and tracking data

Service Related Data

  • WhatsApp API details (related to WhatsApp services)
  • Messages / automation activity (only for service purposes)
  • Leads & campaign data (if shared voluntarily)

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for profit.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide WhatsApp API & automation services

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your information from:
