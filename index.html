FAQ

Questions people ask before signing up

Is Simple Floww the official WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. We're a Meta Tech Provider, and your messages go through Meta's official WhatsApp Business API. We don't use unofficial senders or browser tricks.

Will my number get banned?

Meta doesn't ban numbers for using the official API. It does limit numbers that send spam or get blocked by lots of people. We'll help you follow the rules: message people who've opted in, use approved templates and keep an eye on your quality rating.

Can I use my current WhatsApp number?

In most cases, yes. Depending on how it's set up today, the number may need to move off the regular WhatsApp Business app. Our team explains your options before anything is connected.

How much does a message cost?

You pay Meta's official per-message rate for India from your wallet, with zero markup from us. The rate depends on the message type. See the pricing page for plan details.

What do I need to get started?

A phone number for WhatsApp, a Facebook Business account and basic business documents for Meta's verification. We'll guide you through each step.

Can I message anyone I want?

No. WhatsApp only lets businesses message people who've agreed to hear from them. The first message to a customer has to use a template that Meta has approved.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 7 days.

Do you support Hindi?

Yes. Demos and support are available in Hindi and English.

Can I control what my team can see and do?

Yes. Give each person a role. You decide who can export contacts, launch campaigns or see billing, and you can hide customer phone numbers from your team with privacy mode.

How do I get help if I get stuck?

Click Help on any screen of the panel. Our AI help chat answers right away, and you can talk to our team in the same chat or raise a support ticket and track it. Short videos and step-by-step articles cover every feature.

Do I need a separate CRM?

No. Simple Floww has a full CRM built in: a pipeline board with deal value, an activity timeline, tasks, follow-up reminders, lead scores, a call log, custom fields, lead sources, auto-assign, lost reasons, sales reports, and Excel import and export. It is made for teams that sell over WhatsApp.

What don't you offer?

We don't offer unofficial bulk WhatsApp senders, WhatsApp calling, or Instagram and Facebook DMs.
